# Video Editing Planner

Suggests editing structure, scene cuts, transitions, and pacing for improved video content quality and engagement.

## Activation

- User says "plan the edits for", "how should I cut this", "editing suggestions"
- User has raw footage and needs editing structure
- User wants pacing and transition guidance

## Process

### 1. Scene Breakdown
- Identify natural scene boundaries
- Mark key moments to keep vs cut

### 2. Transitions
- Suggest cut types (hard cut, J-cut, L-cut, dissolve)
- Match transition style to content mood

### 3. Pacing
- Identify slow spots to tighten
- Note where B-roll or graphics should cover gaps
- Rhythm: fast for energy, slow for emphasis

## Output

- Scene-by-scene editing notes
- Transition suggestions per cut point
- Pacing recommendations
- B-roll or overlay suggestions
- Estimated final duration
